Flags:
  • HOW TO — new this week: Back Squat — bar on upper traps (high bar), feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out. Brace core, sit hips back and down, knees track over toes. Depth: thighs to parallel or just below, stop short if the knee or foot protests. Drive up through the whole foot, not just toes. Priority this week is technique and how the knee/foot respond to axial loading, not the weight. Bulgarian Split Squat — rear foot up on a bench, laces down. Front foot far enough forward that the front knee stays roughly over the ankle at the bottom, not pushing past the toes. Torso upright, lower straight down until the back knee is close to the floor, drive up through the front heel. Eccentric Heel Drops — stand on a step edge, ball of foot on the step, heel hanging off. Rise up on both feet, shift weight onto the working foot, then lower that heel SLOWLY over 3-4 seconds below the step level. Bodyweight only this week — +10kg rucksack from Week 10, +15kg from Week 12. Mild discomfort during is fine; pain afterward means dial it back. Ankle Hops — both feet together, small low hops off the balls of the feet. Keep ground contact time as short as possible and stay stiff through the ankle — this is not a big bouncy jump, it's quick and springy. Farmer's Walk on Toes — walk 20m on the balls of both feet with the arch actively domed and core braced, then 20m walking on the heels with toes lifted, then 20m on the outer edges of the feet.
