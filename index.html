REBUILT 3 Sep 2026 — upper-body redesign, targeting rounded shoulders + forward head posture, plus arm/upper-chest definition:

More natural shoulder path than flat barbell bench, less anterior shoulder stress, and hits upper chest more directly — matches both the posture goal and the visible-definition goal as body fat drops. Independent dumbbells also expose/correct left-right imbalance a barbell can mask.
  • Bench Press kept FLAT deliberately, not moved to incline — avoids duplicating Incline DB Press's angle. Together: flat Bench (overall chest, the lift Rob wants) + Incline DB (upper chest specifically) = complete coverage without redundancy.
  • OHP and Bench Press ROOT CAUSE FOUND: Rob's last Bench Press struggle traced to doing it right after OHP — pre-fatigued front delts/triceps from a heavy overhead press, not a strength or shoulder-pain issue (confirmed no pain). Fixed by never sequencing the two presses back-to-back — full order below alternates push/pull throughout, not just around this one pair, so every heavy press gets genuine recovery via pulling work first.
  • ORDER: OHP placed before Bench Press (not after) — deliberate, not arbitrary. OHP is the harder, more shoulder-sensitive lift (rotator cuff flare history), so it goes first while freshest; Bench Press second is more forgiving of slightly reduced freshness. Rob confirmed this explicitly 3 Sep 2026 after discussing both orderings.
